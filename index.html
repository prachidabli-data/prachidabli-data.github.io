About Me

Where business meets the data

I sit at the intersection of commercial thinking, analytics, and business intelligence — and I care most about the decision the data is meant to support.

I am a commercial and business intelligence analyst with a strong quantitative foundation and a few years of experience across data and customer-facing roles. What ties my work together is a simple belief: analysis only has value when someone can act on it.

On the data side, I work across the full workflow — writing SQL to extract and shape data, cleaning and modelling complex datasets, and building dashboards in Power BI and Tableau that translate numbers into something a non-technical stakeholder can actually use. I enjoy the part most people find tedious: making a dataset trustworthy, finding the inconsistency, and understanding why it went wrong.

On the commercial side, working directly with customers and operational teams has taught me to listen properly, communicate under pressure, and connect every piece of analysis back to a business outcome — whether that is cost, efficiency, retention, or revenue.

Right now I am building this portfolio to demonstrate that combination in practice: live freelance projects, professional analytical work, academic projects spanning business analytics and statistical modelling, and self-directed projects on real UK datasets.

Outside of work, I am a digital artist and I am genuinely curious about how AI and modern tooling are reshaping the way analysts work — something I actively explore and bring into my workflow.

How I Work

From a business question to a trusted answer

A structured, repeatable approach I follow on every analytical project — built to keep work aligned, accurate, and genuinely useful.

1

Understand the question

I start with stakeholders, not data — clarifying the decision that needs making, what would change their mind, and what success looks like.

2

Scope & document

I document requirements clearly: data sources, tables, keys, and supporting material, then agree a realistic timeline and deliverables.

3

Extract & transform

I pull the data using SQL, join across tables, and map in supporting fields — building reliable, repeatable data flows.

4

Clean & validate

I remove duplicates, resolve missing values and inconsistent formats, and validate every calculation before anything is trusted.

5

Model & visualise

I build clear data models and dashboards in Power BI or Tableau, designed around the audience — not the analyst.

6

Tell the story

I translate the analysis into a clear narrative and recommendations, then hand over with documentation others can build on.
