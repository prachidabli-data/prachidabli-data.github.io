8. TWO-SAMPLE T-TEST: attendance >= 85% vs < 85%
======================================================================
Caveat: 85% is an arbitrary cut point chosen only to illustrate a
two-sample test. Dichotomizing a continuous variable discards
information and can understate or distort the relationship; the
continuous regression coefficient on attendance_rate in section 5
(and the Pearson correlation in section 3) is the more complete and
statistically preferable summary of this relationship. The t-test
below is included as a worked example of the technique, not as the
primary evidence for an attendance effect.
High-attendance group: n = 135, mean = 77.81, sd = 9.87
Low-attendance group:  n = 185, mean = 67.81, sd = 11.39
Welch's t-test: t = 8.386, p = 1.816e-15
-> Statistically significant difference (p < .05)
Mean difference = 10.00 points, 95% CI = [7.66, 12.34]
